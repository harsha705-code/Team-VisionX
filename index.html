Healthy Round Donut Cell vs. Stiff Crescent Banana Cell

Healthy Blood Cell

Soft Round Donut Cell (Biconcave)

Full of smooth hemoglobin liquid. It is so flexible it squeezes through microscopic capillaries without getting stuck.

  • Lives a full 120 days in circulation
  • Glides smoothly through capillary beds
  • Delivers rich oxygen to brain and vital organs
Sickle Cell (Damaged)

Stiff Crescent Banana Cell

Because of that 1-letter DNA typo, hemoglobin molecules polymerize into rigid rods that bend the erythrocyte into a sharp crescent.

  • Prematurely lyses in 10-20 days (severe anemia)
  • Gets jammed in capillaries, causing vaso-occlusive pain crises
  • Deprives distal organs of arterial oxygenation
✨ What happens after CRISPR cure? The bone marrow permanently stops producing sickled crescents! Newly differentiated erythrocytes emerge as smooth, flexible discs for life.
